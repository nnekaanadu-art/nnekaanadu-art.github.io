## Product Lifecycle Approach

My work follows an iterative product lifecycle:

1. **Discover:** Identify customer needs, business problems, and opportunities.
2. **Strategize:** Evaluate trade-offs, establish priorities, and align roadmaps with capacity.
3. **Define:** Translate needs into clear, actionable, and testable requirements.
4. **Build & Validate:** Develop prototypes, test solutions, and refine them through feedback.
5. **Measure:** Evaluate performance, benefits realization, and business outcomes.
6. **Optimize:** Apply insights to continuously improve products and processes.

These stages are interconnected. Discovery, planning, delivery, and measurement inform one another throughout the product lifecycle.

## How I Work

My approach combines product management principles with hands-on experimentation and AI-assisted development.

**Start with a problem**

Identify a real need, understand the context, and define what a successful solution should achieve.

**Build with AI**

Use tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google Colab to translate requirements into working prototypes and explore potential solutions.

**Test and improve**

Evaluate results against defined criteria, identify limitations, and refine solutions through iterative testing and feedback.

**Automate the routine**

Explore tools such as n8n, Make, and Zapier to connect workflows, reduce repetitive tasks, and improve operational efficiency.
