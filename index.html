AI and customer service

Contact Centre Agent Assist Prototype

An independent concept showing how AI could help contact centre agents after a call. It produces a conversation summary, identifies customer intent, flags complaints and vulnerable customers, and suggests a next best action. Results are checked against a sample answer key, and the prototype matched it on 19 of 20 test calls. Built with Claude, Gemini, Google Colab, and Google Sheets.

Product operations

Northstar: Product Prioritization and Roadmap Tool

A tool that helps product teams decide what to build first. It ranks initiatives using three scoring methods (weighted value, RICE, and WSJF), lays out a quarterly roadmap, checks plans against team capacity, compares scenarios, and produces an executive summary.

Value realization

Value Ledger: Benefits Realization Tracker

Helps product and project leaders track whether delivered work produces the value it promised. It records initiatives and their expected benefits, compares planned and actual results by month and by quarter, tracks net financial value against program costs, and produces a one-page executive brief.
