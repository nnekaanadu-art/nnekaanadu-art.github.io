Product deliveryPublished

AI Story Studio: User Story Drafting and Review

AI-assisted story drafting, acceptance criteria and human review.

About this prototype

Shows how AI can speed up user story writing while people stay in control of quality. It turns a business need into a draft user story, acceptance criteria, and open questions, then guides a human review against the INVEST principles and a Definition of Ready. It also suggests separately scoped follow-up stories and summarizes progress in an executive brief. Story generation is simulated, so the prototype runs anywhere without setup.

Where this applies: backlog refinement, when teams want faster first drafts without skipping the review that makes a story ready for development.

Product operationsPublished

Northstar: Product Prioritization and Roadmap Tool

Prioritize initiatives, model capacity and plan roadmaps.

About this prototype

A tool that helps product teams decide what to build first. It ranks initiatives using three scoring methods (weighted value, RICE, and WSJF), lays out a quarterly roadmap, checks plans against team capacity, compares scenarios, and produces an executive summary.

Where this applies: quarterly planning, when teams need a transparent and consistent way to rank competing initiatives.

Value Ledger: Benefits Realization Tracker

Compare expected and actual benefits and measure business value.

About this prototype

Helps product and project leaders track whether delivered work produces the value it promised. It records initiatives and their expected benefits, compares planned and actual results by month and by quarter, tracks net financial value against program costs, and produces a one-page executive brief.

Where this applies: portfolio reviews that need to show whether funded work delivered the value it promised.

AI and customer serviceProof of concept

Contact Centre Agent Assist Proof of Concept

Explore AI-supported contact-centre workflows and evaluation.

About this prototype

An independent concept showing how AI could help contact centre agents after a call. It produces a conversation summary, identifies customer intent, flags complaints and vulnerable customers, and suggests a next best action. Results are checked against a sample answer key, and the prototype matched it on 19 of 20 test calls. Built with Claude, Gemini, Google Colab, and Google Sheets.

Where this applies: contact centres that want faster after-call work and consistent flagging of complaints and vulnerable customers, with accuracy measured before rollout.

Mortgage Lab: Mortgage Payment Calculator

Explore mortgage scenarios and amortization for Canada and the US.

About this prototype

Helps buyers estimate Canadian and US mortgage payments, compare two scenarios side by side, view amortization schedules, and estimate first-time buyer incentives in Ontario and British Columbia. For illustration only. Not financial advice.

Where this applies: customer-facing tools that help buyers understand their costs before speaking with an advisor.
